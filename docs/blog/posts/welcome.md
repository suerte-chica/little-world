# Welcome to Little World 🌍

Hello and welcome! I'm thrilled to launch **Little World** — a small corner of the internet dedicated to collecting and sharing the interesting, the beautiful, and the surprising things I come across.

<!-- more -->

## What to expect

This blog will cover a wide range of topics — there's no strict editorial line here, just genuine curiosity:

- **Travel stories** — places I've been, things I've seen, and tips for exploring
- **Nature** — wildlife encounters, natural wonders, and the outdoors
- **Culture** — food, art, music, traditions from near and far
- **Ideas** — concepts and rabbit holes worth exploring

## How to follow along

You can subscribe to the RSS feed (linked in the footer) to get notified whenever a new post goes live.

## A small invitation

If you come across something interesting and think it belongs here, [get in touch](../../contact.md) — I'd love to hear about it.

Thanks for stopping by. Here's to collecting little pieces of the world, one post at a time. 🌿
